How this career-through-age leaderboard works

“Through age 30” includes MLB seasons with a season-level age of 30 or younger. The live board adds the hits from every qualifying season and ranks each player by the resulting total.

This question rewards players who reached the majors early, stayed healthy, and accumulated a large number of at-bats. Change the controls to compare active players, franchises, narrower eras, or another age.

The result measures accumulated hits through an age cutoff. It does not adjust for season length, league run environment, or historical schedule differences.
