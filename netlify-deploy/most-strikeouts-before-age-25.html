How this young-pitcher leaderboard works

“Before age 25” includes MLB seasons in which a pitcher’s season-level age is 24 or younger. The live board combines strikeouts from every qualifying season into one total for each pitcher.

Use the result to compare early-career workload and strikeout accumulation across eras. The controls can limit the pool by franchise, season range, active status, and minimum innings.

Strikeout environments and pitcher usage have changed substantially across baseball history. Treat the total as an age-based record, not an era-adjusted talent ranking.
