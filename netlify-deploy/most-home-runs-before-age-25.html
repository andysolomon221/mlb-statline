How this age leaderboard works

“Before age 25” includes MLB seasons in which a player’s season-level age is 24 or younger. The live board combines each qualifying season into one player total and ranks hitters by home runs.

The interactive result covers seasons from 1901 through 2026 and can be narrowed to active players, a franchise, a different era, or another age cutoff.

Age is based on the season value supplied with public MLB records. This is an accumulated career-through-age question, not a single-season leaderboard.
