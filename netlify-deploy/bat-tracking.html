How to Read It

Measured by Statcast beginning in 2023

Bat speed is measured at the sweet spot. A fast swing is 75+ mph. Squared-up rate measures realized exit velocity versus the maximum possible; blasts combine strong contact with fast bat speed. Swords credit pitchers for awkward, noncompetitive swings. Public coverage begins in the second half of 2023, so these are modern-era leaderboards rather than all-time records.
